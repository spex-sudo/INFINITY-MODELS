[Aspiring and emerging models, ages 16–30. What's covered

[The Walk (Runway Mechanics): Learn the anatomy of a commanding runway walk. The Book (Portfolio Building on a Budget): You don't need a massive budget for test shoots to get noticed. Learn how to capture industry-standard digitals (polaroids), curate a clean, high-contrast portfolio, and leverage what you already have to attract agencies. The Business (Industry Secrets): Mastering casting etiquette, marketing yourself on social media to build authority, and developing a professional reputation that makes directors want to re-book you.]
